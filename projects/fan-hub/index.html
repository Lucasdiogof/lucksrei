Goiás App

An app for Goiás fans: matches, club, content, a mini-games Arena and the Fans' Lineup in one place.

Android iOS Web / PWA Flutter Supabase
Privacy — Goiás EC
Step 01

Welcome to Goiás

Sign in or create an account and land on Home, with the next match and the fan's shortcuts.

Step 02

Follow the team

Round fixtures, calendar, standings and other competitions, all in the Matches tab.

Step 03

Get to know the club

The club's history, titles, idols, squad, anthem and documents, plus news and social feeds in the Media tab.

Step 04

Live the club

Membership plans, tickets and the store. Purchases run in demo mode, with no real charge.

Step 05

Join the Arena

Games and challenges, the fans' lineup, the match passport and the ranking, in the Arena Esmeraldina.

Architecture

How the app is built

Layered Clean Architecture (domain, data and presentation), with Supabase as the backend and RLS policies protecting each fan's data.

App core: Flutter · Clean Architecture · Arena engine · Design system
