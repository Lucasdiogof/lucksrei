Bebômetro

An app to organise nights out: record drinks and orders per person, note expenses and split the bill with your friends.

Open in yourBrowserDownload on theApp StoreGet it onGoogle Play

Product overview

Bebômetro keeps the whole night in one place: who is at the table, what each person ordered, what it cost and how to split it. The account is personal and syncs your records; the people at the table do not need an account.

Step 01

Join Bebômetro

Create an account or sign in with email and password, then start from the home screen.

Step 02

Start the outing and set the table

Choose the kind of outing and the place, follow the time, the budget and what each person ordered, and see everything on an illustrated table.

Step 03

Log the drinks

Pick from the catalog with the source of each data point, create your own drinks, order for each person or for a shared bottle, and repeat with one tap.

Step 04

Bill and split

Service charge, discount and extras, split equally, by item or with custom amounts, and a text summary to share. The app does not charge or handle Pix.

Step 05

Wrap up and look back

Finish the outing, read the summary and the history, and follow your statistics over time.

Step 06

Games and settings

Offline games for the table, a beer glass you tilt and a pretend breathalyzer, plus language and preferences.

Key features

OutingsCreate an outing with place, times and an optional budget, and add a short note afterwards.
Drinks and orders per personRecord each drink with volume, strength and price, from a built-in catalogue of drinks and recipes or your own.
Tab and splittingAdd extra items, service charge and discount, split the bill and note who has paid. People at the table are just nicknames.
Labels and menusPhotograph a label or a menu and let the app read the text on your device to fill in the drink.
Places and mapSearch for bars and restaurants, save places and, if you want, use your location for a single search.
Table gamesCards for the table (with an adults-only mode you confirm), a joke breathalyser and a beer tilt simulator.
Your dataSync with your account, export your records as JSON and CSV, and delete your account inside the app.
LanguagesPortuguese, English and Spanish.

The breathalyser and the beer simulator are jokes: they do not measure alcohol, do not calculate blood alcohol and do not say whether anyone can drive. If you drank, do not drive. Bebômetro is intended for people over 18.
