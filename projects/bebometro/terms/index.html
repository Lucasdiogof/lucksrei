Terms of use — Bebômetro

The rules for using Bebômetro, in plain language.

Updated October 20, 2026. It describes how the app works today and is not legal advice.

1. Who we are and what you accept

Bebômetro is published by Lucksrei (LUCAS DIOGO FRANCA, CNPJ 54.868.173/0001-55, Brazil). By creating an account and using the app you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. The service

Bebômetro is for organising nights out: recording drinks and orders per person, noting expenses and splitting the bill. The account is personal and required to use the app; it is used to store and sync your records. The app is free and, in this version, has no subscription or in-app purchases.

3. Your account

Give an e-mail that is yours and keep your password safe. You are responsible for what happens in your account. If you lose access, use the password recovery by code in the app or contact us.

4. The breathalyser and simulator are a joke

The app's breathalyser and beer simulator are recreational. Results are random or visual: they do not measure alcohol, do not calculate blood alcohol and do not tell you whether you are fit to drive. Never use the app to decide whether you can drive, operate machinery or make any safety or health decision. If you drank, do not drive.

5. Alcohol and responsibility

Bebômetro is intended for people over 18 and does not encourage drinking. The app's records and totals are informational and depend on what you type. Drink in moderation and respect other people's limits. The “+18” mode of the games requires that everyone playing is an adult and agrees to take part; everything can be skipped.

6. Your content and other people

You are responsible for what you enter, including nicknames of other people at the table and photos of labels and menus. Enter only what you have a reason to record and do not use the app to expose, harass or harm anyone.

7. Permitted use

Do not try to access other people's accounts or data, bypass security, overload the service or use the app unlawfully.

8. Your data

Your records are synced with your account, and you can export them and delete the account at any time, as explained in the privacy policy and on the account and data deletion page.

9. Intellectual property

The app, the Bebômetro brand and the catalogue of drinks, recipes and game cards belong to Lucksrei or are used with permission. The records you create remain yours.

10. Availability and liability

The app is provided as is, with no guarantee that it will run without interruption or contain all information. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for damages arising from use of the app, including decisions made from the totals, from the breathalyser or simulator results, or loss of records due to a device or connection failure.

11. Ending use

You can stop using the app and delete your account whenever you like. We may suspend accounts that violate these terms.

12. Changes and governing law

We may update these terms; the date on this page shows the version in force. The laws of Brazil apply.
