OrbiJob

A global job search app: browse public listings from many sources, save favourites and searches with alerts, and keep track of your applications.

AndroidiOSWebFlutterSupabase
Open the web app

Product overview

OrbiJob gathers public job listings from third-party sources into one catalogue you can use without an account. With an account you can save what matters, keep a professional profile and resumes, and follow each application. OrbiJob is not an employer or a recruiter, and it never sends applications for you.

Step 01

Join OrbiJob

Create an account or sign in. You can also explore jobs without one.

Step 02

Find jobs worldwide

Search by profession, country and work mode, narrow it with filters and open each job's details.

Step 03

Recommendations and alerts

See why a job is recommended, save searches and get notified when new matching jobs appear.

Step 04

Follow your applications

Prepare with the application kit, track each stage and compare jobs. You decide the stage; OrbiJob never contacts the employer.

Step 05

Your profile and résumés

Keep your experience, skills and PDF résumés in one profile; the app reads the text on your device and you review before anything is applied.

Step 06

Make it yours

Language and appearance in one place.

Key features

One cataloguePublic listings from many sources, shown with their source and licence, searchable without an account.
Favourites and filtersSave jobs with a note, hide the ones you do not want and see which you have already viewed.
Saved searches and alertsTurn on alerts for a search and get in-app and push notifications about new matching jobs.
Profile and resumesKeep your experience, education, skills and PDF resumes (up to 5 MiB) in one profile.
Application kitCopy fields from your profile to paste on the employer's site. OrbiJob never fills in or submits forms for you.
Application trackingRecord the stage, notes and reminders of each application you decide to track.
TranslationTranslate titles and descriptions: on Android and iOS it runs on your device.
Your dataExport a ZIP with your data and resumes, and delete your account inside the app.

OrbiJob is not an employer, recruiter or employment agency and does not guarantee a job. Listings are published by third parties; check the details on the employer's site before applying.
