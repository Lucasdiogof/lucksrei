Terms of use — OrbiJob

The rules for using OrbiJob.

Technical draft, last updated 2026-10-14; not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

By using OrbiJob you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. The service

OrbiJob is not an employer, recruiter or employment agency, does not take part in hiring decisions and does not send applications on your behalf.

3. Listings come from third parties

Listings, salaries, locations and requirements are published by employers and sources outside our control. We try to show them faithfully and to hide listings that are no longer open, but we do not guarantee that a listing is accurate, current, available or lawful, nor that you are eligible for it (including visas, registrations or certifications). Check the details on the employer's site before applying. Some sources require attribution or have their own licence; they are listed in the app's licences page.

4. Your account

You must give a valid e-mail address, keep your password confidential and are responsible for what happens under your account. You can delete your account at any time in the app.

5. Your content

The profile, resumes, notes and other content you add remain yours. You allow OrbiJob to store and process them only to provide the app's features to you. Upload only resumes and information that you are entitled to share.

6. Acceptable use

  • Do not break the law or the rights of others.
  • Do not try to access other people's data, disrupt the service, or overload it with automated requests.
  • Do not copy the catalogue in bulk or resell it. Listings keep the rights and licences of their sources.

The OrbiJob name, logo, design and software belong to Lucksrei, except where third-party or open-source components carry their own licences.

8. Availability and changes

The app is provided free of charge and "as is". Features, sources and these terms may change, and the service may be interrupted. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service.
