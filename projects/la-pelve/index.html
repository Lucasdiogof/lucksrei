La Pelve

A clinical organization tool for pelvic physiotherapy professionals, covering scheduling, patients and the full appointment history end to end.

Android iOS Flutter Supabase
App privacy policy

Product overview

La Pelve is an organization tool for the daily clinical routine of pelvic physiotherapists: appointment scheduling, patient records, anamnesis, clinical progress, appointment history and basic financial records (payments), all in one place.

Key features

ScheduleOrganization of time slots and appointments.
PatientsRecords and history per patient.
AnamnesisStructured record of the initial assessment.
Progress notesRecord of clinical progress throughout treatment.
Appointment historyTimeline of sessions per patient.
Payment recordsPayments and a monthly report of received amounts.

Tech stack

Mobile

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Android
  • iOS

Backend

  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
Real screens

Clinical routine in a single app

Scheduling, patient records and finances integrated, with its own visual identity and available in Portuguese and English.

La Pelve is a clinical organization and record-keeping tool. It does not provide diagnoses, does not prescribe automated treatment and does not replace the professional's clinical judgment. No real patient data is used in this presentation.
