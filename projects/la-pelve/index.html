La Pelve

A clinical management app for pelvic physiotherapists: schedule, patient records, progress notes and payment tracking.

Android iOS Flutter Supabase
App privacy policy

Product overview

Appointment scheduling, patient records, anamnesis, clinical progress notes, history and a basic payment record, all in the same app.

Key features

ScheduleOrganization of time slots and appointments.
PatientsRecords and history per patient.
AnamnesisPatient registration in up to 11 steps: medical history, urinary, sexual and bowel function, an assessment form and the treatment plan.
Progress notesRecord of clinical progress throughout treatment.
Appointment statusScheduled, confirmed, attended, cancelled, no-show or rescheduled, updated right from the schedule.
Payment recordsPayments and a monthly report of received amounts.
AttachmentsPhotos and PDFs per patient; photos open right in the app.
Access controlRLS per professional, private files with signed URLs and a biometric lock on mobile.
Planned

WhatsApp reminders — in development

In development

An integration with WhatsApp Business is in development so that each professional can connect their own WhatsApp account and send appointment reminders to patients who have authorized this contact; patients will be able to confirm the appointment by replying. Patient consent is already recorded in the app; connecting the account and sending reminders are not active yet.

Reminders will be limited to the information needed about the appointment and will not include clinical data, diagnosis, anamnesis, progress notes or any other information from the patient record.

Each professional remains responsible for their own WhatsApp Business account and for the contact authorizations of their patients.

Step 01

Access your clinic

Sign in or create the physiotherapist's account. The app is available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Step 02

Organize your appointments

The schedule shows appointments by day and lets you create a new one.

Step 03

Manage your patients

From the list to the step-by-step registration, with anamnesis and a treatment plan, to the patient's record and progress notes.

Step 04

Run your practice

Financial report and charge records. The WhatsApp integration is still in development.

La Pelve is a clinical organization and record-keeping tool. It does not provide diagnoses, does not prescribe automated treatment and does not replace the professional's clinical judgment. No real patient data is used in this presentation.
