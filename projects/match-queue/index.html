Match Queue

An app for competitive teams to find matches. The matchmaking queue runs on the server; the client only asks and follows.

Flutter Supabase Realtime Edge Functions FCM
App privacy policy

Product overview

Teams organize into groups, join the queue and get a match when a compatible opponent exists. The app covers team creation, invites, the queue, match formation and real-time notifications.

Product

The app in use

Real screens

From queue to match

The team picks a platform, squad and mode, then joins the queue. The whole team follows the search in real time.

Real screens

Central and Market

A catalog of players and clubs, mechanics guides and market prices per platform.

Matchmaking on the server

Pairing has to stay consistent with many clients acting at once, so every decision happens on the server (Postgres + Edge Functions), never in the app: a FIFO queue, a per-team lock (one active search at a time) and a transactional outbox to deliver match events and notifications even when something fails midway.

Team→ Queue (FIFO)→ Server Authority→ Match→ Realtime sync

Key features

Teams & membersTeam creation, invites and member management.
Search queueOne active search per team, with automatic expiration.
Server-side matchmakingPairing decided entirely on the backend.
NotificationsPush via FCM for queue and match events.
Real-time stateSync via Supabase Realtime.
Row Level SecurityData access restricted per team/user in Postgres.
