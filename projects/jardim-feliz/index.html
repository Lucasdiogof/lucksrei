Jardim Feliz

Care for your plants at your own pace: build your garden, log watering and notes, follow the care calendar and learn about each species.

Open in yourBrowser

Product overview

Jardim Feliz helps plant lovers not forget a thing. You add each plant, the app suggests what to check and when, and the journal keeps photos and notes. It runs in the browser (installable as an app) and keeps working offline after the first sign-in. No ads and no tracking.

Step 01

Build your garden

Add each plant with a nickname, species and where it lives; everything shows up in My Garden.

Step 02

Care at the right time

On the plant page, check the soil moisture, log watering and follow the plant's mood.

Step 03

Find out which plant it is

Photograph a leaf or flower and see possible species; you decide which one it is.

Step 04

Learn and organize

A catalog of plants and foods, games to learn and a calendar with the next care tasks.

Key features

My GardenAll your plants in one place, with each one's mood.
Plant pageSoil check, watering log, a journal with photos and notes.
Photo identificationSuggests possible species from a photo; the final choice is yours.
CalendarNext care tasks and reminders for each plant.
LearnCatalog of plants and foods with per-species information.
GamesChallenges and levels to learn about plants by playing.
Works offlineInstallable in the browser and usable without internet after the first sign-in.
PrivacyNo ads, no advertising identifiers and no tracking across apps.

Jardim Feliz is educational and does not replace agronomic advice. The screens shown are from the app in Portuguese.
