Lucksrei Systems — Management system

StockPort System

Inventory, sales and cash register in one system for small businesses: from the stock room to the sale, all connected.

App Store ↗Google Play ↗

Product overview

StockPort System organizes the day of a small shop: products and stock, point of sale, cash register, customers and suppliers, team and reports in a single app. Each company signs in to the same app and only sees its own data. Access is by software licence.

Step 01

Register your products

Products with category, barcode, sale price, cost and minimum stock. Find them by name or read the barcode with the camera or a scanner.

Step 02

Control the stock

Goods entries from suppliers, adjustments with a reason and the full history of movements. Products below the minimum appear in the restock list.

Step 03

Sell at the point of sale

Cart with quantities, discounts and customer; combined payments in cash, Pix, debit or credit, with change; receipt in PDF. Each sale lowers the stock.

Step 04

Close the cash register

Opening amount, supplies and withdrawals, expected in the drawer, counted amount and difference, with the history of closed registers.

Step 05

Follow your results

Dashboard with today's sales, revenue and estimated profit, low stock and cash drawer; reports by day, by payment method and best sellers, exported to PDF or CSV.

Key features

Products and categoriesPrice, cost, barcode, photo and minimum stock for each product.
Stock and movementsEntries, adjustments, sales and average cost, with the balance always up to date.
Low stockProducts below the minimum are highlighted and listed for restocking.
Point of saleSearch or scan, cart, discounts, customer, combined payments, change and PDF receipt.
Cash registerOpening, supplies, withdrawals, expected amount, closing and history.
Customers and suppliersContacts with purchase history and goods entry history.
Team and permissionsOwner, administrator, manager, cashier and view-only roles; costs and profit only for those allowed.
Reports and exportsRevenue by day and payment method, best sellers, estimated profit and margin; PDF and CSV.
Data per companyEach company only sees its own data, protected on the server.
Your language, your themePortuguese, English and Spanish; light and dark themes; Android, iOS and Web.

StockPort System is available on Google Play and the App Store. The video uses demonstrative data. The system does not issue fiscal invoices (NF-e) and has no bank or marketplace integration.
