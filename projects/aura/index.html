Aprovaura

A study app for entrance exams and tests, with questions by subject, mock exams, mistake review and essay grading.

Android iOS Flutter Supabase IA
App privacy policy

Product overview

Aprovaura brings questions, mock exams, mistake review and essays into a single study flow. Students pick a subject, answer, and track their progress.

Content structure

Questions are organized by subject and topic. Progress is recorded per question and totaled by area, so students can see where they miss the most.

Key features

Question bankQuestions and answers organized by subject.
Study sessionsPractice flow focused by topic.
Aura progressionThe app's score: students advance by subject and by question.
Custom mock examsSubjects, difficulty and number of questions of your choice, with exam mode.
Mistake reviewWrong answers grouped by subject and topic.
AI essay gradingEstimated grading by competency (Gemini), with a privacy notice.
Interactive mapsGeography activities with map location.
Daily goal and streakGoal for the day and a streak of study days.
Three languages, two themesPortuguese, English and Spanish; light and dark theme.
Cross-platformThe same app on Android and iOS.

Study

Practice

Students pick a subject or do a quick practice. The answer shows up right away and mistakes go to a review area.

Mock exams

Build your own mock exam

Students combine subjects, choose the difficulty of each and set how many questions they want. In exam mode, the result is only shown at the end.

Essay

AI essay grading

Students pick a topic, write the text and submit it for grading. The feedback is estimated by AI, following ENEM's five competencies, and the app makes clear it is not the official score.

Maps

Geography maps

In Brazilian Relief, students locate landforms on the map. Correct answers earn Aura and the final screen shows performance.

Progress

Daily goal and Aura

The home screen shows the day's goal and the streak. Aura is the app's score and sets the student's level. There are light and dark themes, and the app is available in Portuguese, English and Spanish.
