About

Senior Mobile Developer · Flutter Engineer

Flutter · Android · iOS · Architecture · Delivery

I build and maintain Flutter applications for Android and iOS with a focus on architecture, scalability and production operations. I work with Clean Architecture, TDD, BLoC/Cubit, dependency injection and feature-first organization, including migrating native applications to Flutter.

My work doesn't stop at the interface. I handle data and integrations (REST APIs, JWT, Firebase, Supabase/PostgreSQL, realtime, offline-first apps with sync) and the infrastructure behind delivery: multi-flavor architectures, environments, CI/CD with Fastlane, GitHub Actions, CodeMagic and Azure DevOps, build signing, and publishing through TestFlight, App Store Connect and Google Play Console.

I've worked in fintech, agritech, government systems and my own products, with requirements such as financial transactions, real-time geolocation, face match, chat and offline sync. My earlier Java/Spring backend experience completes the picture and lets me see the product end to end, from architecture and data to the build that reaches production.
